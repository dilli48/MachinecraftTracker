Precision EMS Assembly Line

Quality is engineered directly into our production workflow. We operate specialized Electronics Manufacturing Services (EMS) equipment handled by qualified engineering staff to guarantee zero-defect manufacturing.

1

Solder Paste Stenciling

Automated, high-precision solder stencil application.

2

SMT Component Pick & Place

High-speed, accurate component routing and placement.

3

Reflow Thermal Oven

Controlled thermal profiling for perfect solder bonding.

4

Wave Soldering Line

Superior bonding for heavy-duty through-hole components.

5

Ultrasonic Cleaning

Complete removal of flux and particulate residue.

Rigorous Testing Protocol

Every circuit board that leaves our facility undergoes strict validation to exceed ISO 9001:2015 standards:

Automated Optical Inspection (AOI)

Automated visual checks for microscopic component alignment, polarity verification, and solder joint integrity.

Burn-In Chamber Testing

Extreme thermal stress cycling to eliminate early-life component failures and guarantee field durability.

Endurance Stress Testing

Prolonged operational stress cycling under load to guarantee lifetime performance in harsh industrial environments.
